DGCA Passenger Refund Rights (India)

24-Hour Zero-Fee Cancellation Window

Under DGCA CAR Section 3, passengers are entitled to a full refund without cancellation fees if cancelled within 24 hours of booking (provided departure is >7 days away).

Flight Delay > 2 Hours Full Refund Right

If airline reschedules or delays by over 2 hours, passengers can claim an immediate 100% refund to the original payment method without deductions.

Turnaround SLA (Credit Cards vs. UPI)

Airlines must process refunds within 7 working days for credit cards and 3 working days for UPI / Net Banking.
